THE PARTY QUIZ
THE QUICK VERSION

Big brains. Bragging rights.

  1. Get your crew together.One person hosts. Everyone else joins with the room code. 2–8 players.
  2. Eight questions. Fifteen seconds each.Pick your answer and lock it in before the timer runs out.
  3. Back your brain.Wager 1, 2, or 3 points. Right answers win your wager; wrong answers lose it. Your score never drops below zero.
  4. Top score takes it.See the answer and standings after every round. Ties share the win.
